Why it helps and what was checked

Aggregate by customer key/year before fetching wide customer attributes, regroup by all original customer fields, then retain the four original self-joins and ratio expressions. All 100 ordered rows and all 1292 unlimited output rows match exactly.

The nonempty fixture covers duplicate customer IDs, original attribute grouping, missing groups, NULLs, discounts and date joins. The historical pivot's duplicate-ID assumption is documented; current SF4 IDs are unique. The new rewrite preserves the broader original semantics.
