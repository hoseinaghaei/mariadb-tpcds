Why it is faster

Scan customers in output order, probe the largest qualifying store/web amount through customer/net-paid/date indexes, and stop after 100 customers satisfy the original conditions. A runtime uniqueness guard selects this path only when business IDs are unique and non-NULL. Otherwise the original query supplies the result. The streaming guard and selective eligibility checks reduce overhead further.

All 100 ordered rows match. All 1228 unlimited output rows also match, and fixtures validate unique-ID fast execution, duplicate-ID fallback, empty results, NULLs, dates, negative second-year values and original ratios.

The large gain depends on this top-100 query instance. It is not a claim about every customer, every dataset or official TPC-DS performance. Duplicate-ID data correctly falls back to the slower original strategy.
